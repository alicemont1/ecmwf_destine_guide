## Extremes Digital Twin

Provides information on extreme events on a timescale of a few days ahead, at very high spatial resolution.

---

The Extremes Digital Twin consists of two main components:

### Global Component (Continuous)

- Developed by ECMWF, producing global simulations with:
  - **4.4 km atmosphere resolution**
  - **25 km ocean resolution**
- Forecasts available up to **4 days ahead**
- Initialized daily from the **00UTC ECMWF operational analysis (IFS)**
- Uses global models: **IFS-NEMO**

:::{warning}
Forecasts can only be retrieved for start dates up to **15 days** before the current date.
:::


---

### Regional Component (On-demand)

:::{important}
In development – not yet available
:::

- Procured by ECMWF and developed in partnership led by **Météo-France** alongside several European institutions, including national meteorological services (NMS).
- Produces high-resolution regional simulations over Europe at **500-700 meter resolution**
- Provides forecasts up to **2 days in advance**
- Runs **on demand**
- Based on high-resolution models developed within the **ACCORD consortium** (ALARO, AROME, HARMOY-AROME)


:::{note}
Impact-sector models for floods, air quality, and renewable energy will be integrated into the workflows of both the global and regional extremes components.
:::
