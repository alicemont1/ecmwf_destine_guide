# Climate Digital Twin

The Climate Digital twin models produce climate projections over multi-decadal time periods at 5-10km resolution.

These models are being developed through a collaboration led by Finland’s IT Center for Science (CSC), involving climate institutions, supercomputing centers, national meteorological services, academia, and industrial partners.

The objectives of the Climate DT are:
* operationalize climate projections on a regular basis (yearly)
* Provide on-demand simulations to explore “what if” scenarios
* Establish an operational framework encompassing monitoring, evaluation, quality assurance, and uncertainty quantification

Three different global climate models are being used to run the simulations. They include:
* **ICON**: The ICOsahedral Non-hydrostatic model, developed by DWD, MPI-M, DKRZ, KIT, and C2SM
* **IFS-NEMO**: ECMWF’s IFS coupled with the NEMO ocean model, implemented by BSC with ECMWF
* **IFS-FESOM**: ECMWF’s IFS coupled with the Alfred Wegener Institute’s Finite-VolumE Sea ice–Ocean Model (FESOM)


## The Simulations

The experimental design is inspired by the High Resolution Model Intercomparison Project (HighResMIP), part of CMIP6. HighResMIP defines three core experiments: a control run with fixed forcings, historical runs with observed changes, and future scenario runs under increasing greenhouse gases. Climate DT follows a similar structure, adapting these protocols to kilometre-scale models, and extends them with additional storyline simulations.


### Historical Simulations

Starting in 1990, the forcing follows observed changes in greenhouse gases, aerosols etc. until 2020. The simulations are using standardised CMIP6 forcing.

### Control Experiment

In this experiment, all external forcings are held constant at their 1990 values, including greenhouse gases, aerosols, ozone, solar forcing, etc. This setup provides a stationary climate background to test the stability of the high-resolution model and to detect artificial drift, serving as a quality-control reference for the historical and scenario simulations.

### Future Projections

Future scenarios are based on the **Scenario Model Intercomparison Project (ScenarioMIP)**. DestinE currently uses **SSP3-7.0**, a high-emissions pathway marked by limited international cooperation and weak climate policy. Simulations start in 2020 from reanalysis data, include a five-year ocean spin-up, and extend forward under SSP3-7.0. Later phases will add more scenarios.


### Storyline Simulations (Story-Nudging)

Storyline simulations provide a *what-if* framework to explore how recent weather events might look under different climate conditions—such as a warmer world or pre-industrial times.

In these simulations, the model is nudged to follow observed large-scale atmospheric circulation patterns from ERA5 data (2017–2023), effectively reproducing real-world atmospheric dynamics. This means that extreme events driven by large-scale circulation are realistically captured, as they occurred in reality. Meanwhile, smaller-scale processes—such as rainfall, temperature changes, and convection—are allowed to evolve freely within the model’s physics.

This setup can then be used to simulate the same events under different climate conditions — such as a colder 1950s climate or a warmer future climate — to explore how those events might have unfolded differently. Would the impacts have changed? Would the event have been more or less intense?

Initial conditions are drawn from:  
- ocean-only spin-up simulations for **1950** and **2017**, and  
- the **IFS–FESOM projection for a +2 °C world (tplus2.0)**. 


## Ocean Spin-Up and Initialization Strategy

To ensure stable starting conditions, the ocean must be brought close to equilibrium before running coupled simulations. 
This is achieved through a combination of ocean-only and coupled spin-up phases, which differ slightly depending on the type of simulation.


### For Historical Simulations

- Ocean initialized from reanalysis in 1985  
- 5-year ocean-only spin-up with ERA5 forcing (1985–1990)  
- 2-year coupled spin-up with fixed 1990 forcing  
- Provides initial conditions for historical runs starting in 1990  

### For Future Projections

- Ocean-only spin-up from 2015–2019 with ERA5 forcing  
- No coupled spin-up; projections start directly from ocean-only state in 2020
